Drag to spin  ·  Click to pulse  ·  Scroll to dive in

An interactive three-dimensional globe of Earth drawn as thousands of glowing dots on a black starfield, slowly rotating. Bright arcs periodically rise from cities and curve to pulsing data-center markers, representing users connecting to data centers around the world. Drag or use arrow keys to spin it, click or press Enter to send a pulse, scroll, pinch or use plus and minus keys to zoom.

This experience needs WebGL

Your browser or device doesn't support WebGL2, so the interactive globe can't start.
